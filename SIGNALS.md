# Who you prospect, and the signals that say "now"

Status: EXAMPLE. Your agent asks you and replaces this line with "Status: yours" once the file is yours.

## Who you sell to

Heads, directors and VPs of Revenue Operations, Sales Operations or GTM Operations at US B2B software companies of 51 to 500 employees.

**Not a fit:** consultants, advisors, fractional and independent roles; sales roles without operations in the title; companies under 51 or over 500 employees.

## The signal searches

One Sales Navigator search per signal, each on the same buyers above. Each gets a daily Evaboot recurring export, so each morning brings only the people who are new to it.

| Signal | Sales Navigator filter | Why it means "now" |
|---|---|---|
| `changed_jobs` | Changed jobs in the last 90 days | A new leader rebuilds the stack and the data in the first months. |
| `posted` | Posted on LinkedIn in the last 30 days | They are active, and you have something to reply to. |

The brief also reads two signals from each person's own data: **under 3 months in role**, and **company growth of 30% or more in a year**.

## What you sell, in one line

Evaboot exports Sales Navigator searches to clean lists with verified emails, and can send each week's new people straight to the CRM.

## The brief

- **10 people a day at most**, best signals first. The rest compete again the next day, for up to 7 days.
- **One next step each:** `email` (verified email), `linkedin` (no safe email, or a reason to connect first), `call`, or `watch` (not yet).
- **The reason** is one line, from their own signals and profile. Never invent a fact.
- **No guessed gender:** never he, she, him or her. Use the first name, "they" or the role.
- A person is in the brief **once**. People judged not a fit are not shown again either.
